Chess, Maths & Magic

Your next move. Nothing in the way.

Play local chess, explore maths and magic, study positions and practice checkmates in Chaturanga. Free browser app. No account needed for local play.

Combat Modes & Variants

  • Classic Chess Standard 8×8 tactical chess against local opponents or customizable engine bots.
  • Chess960 (Fischer Random) Randomized starting pieces on the home ranks with standard castling rules.
  • Fog of War Imperfect information chess where only squares your pieces see or attack are visible.
  • Atomic Chess Captures cause an explosion destroying adjacent non-pawn pieces. Kings cannot capture.
  • Chess MM (Mind & Magic) A mental-maths question before each move, or a 90-second maths break with earned board spells.

Also available: quiet matches against computer bots, position study, practice checkmate drills, and private rooms for classic chess, maths and spells, and 3- or 4-player cross-board house rules.

No account needed for local play.
