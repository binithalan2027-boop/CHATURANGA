## Full description (draft)
Chaturanga is a quiet courtyard for chess and chess-adjacent games.

- Classic chess against the computer or a friend, no account needed at home
- Mind Before Move: answer a quick mental-maths question to unlock each move
- Mantra Duel: a 90-second maths break; the winner earns one of seven powers
- Original three- and four-player cross-board chess on one big board
- Study tools: openings, checkmate practice, local analysis
- Private online rooms with friend challenges and a report/moderation flow

House rules apply in cross-board modes (king capture wins; no check, castling
or en passant). Online maths modes are turn-based, not simultaneous.

No ads. No tracking beyond what the privacy policy lists.
